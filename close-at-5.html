    05

    Why your inbox fills up

    Every question your website doesn't answer turns into a message to you. Here's what's missing on bfamilydesigns.com today. The answer sheet the front desk uses fixes most of it at the same time.

    MissingNo FAQ page

    Prices, sizes, turnaround and pickup only live in product menus, so people ask.

    The answer sheet becomes your FAQ page.

    MissingNo pressing or care instructions

    No temperature, time or peel steps anywhere. That means "what temp?" messages, and peeling complaints when people guess.

    Put press and care steps on every product and in the pickup text.

    MissingNo file guidelines

    The gang sheet builder pages have no description, so nothing tells people to use a transparent 300 DPI PNG.

    Add three lines of file rules to the builder pages.

    MissingNo shipping or reprint policy

    Only a privacy policy is published. "What if it peels?" has no written answer, so every one becomes a conversation.

    Publish short shipping and reprint policies in Shopify settings.

    MissingContact page is a bare form

    No phone, address or hours. People who want to pick up have to message to find out where and when.

    Add the address, pickup hours and a map link.

    BrokenFooter social icons go to Shopify, not you

    The Instagram, TikTok and YouTube icons link to Shopify's own accounts, left over from the theme.

    Point them at your pages, or remove the ones you don't use.

    How it gets built

    Free fixes first, then texts, then the AI desk in draft mode. Nothing goes on autopilot until you've watched it work.

    Night 1about an hour together
    • Write the answer sheet with you: prices, files, pressing, care, pickup, policies.
    • Publish it as an FAQ page, product descriptions, and shipping and reprint policies.
    • Put your address and pickup hours in Shopify's pickup instructions, so every "ready for pickup" email carries them.
    • Turn on Meta's free away message, greeting and FAQs for Messenger and Instagram, and Shopify Inbox instant answers for site chat.
    • Fix the footer social links.
    $0All built into Shopify and Meta.
    Week 1status texts
    • Connect an SMS app to Shopify for the ready-for-pickup, shipped and file-fix texts.
    • Register the business number with the carriers (required for business texting).
    • Texts go only to customers who give a phone number at checkout.
    Low monthlyA small app fee plus pennies per text. We'll price it out with you before anything is bought.
    Week 2AI desk, draft mode
    • The AI reads new Messenger, Instagram and site chat messages and drafts replies from your answer sheet.
    • You approve each draft with one tap. Anything amber waits for you, with the details collected.
    • We fix any answer you don't like in the sheet, once.
    PilotCovered by the free trial period.
    Week 3+your call
    • Switch green topics to auto-send when you trust them.
    • Turn on the "text me right away" rules and the 7 AM summary.
    You decideKeep it, change it, or turn it off.
    What it won't do
    • Promise refunds or reprints. Those always wait for you.
    • Quote custom jobs or rush deadlines. It collects the details and hands them over.
    • Make up answers. If it isn't on the answer sheet, it says you'll follow up.
    • Pretend to be you. It introduces itself as the B Family front desk.
