Three moves, in order

Google says it ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence. You can't move your shop, but you control the other two. Everything below is written and ready. Open any item to copy it.

1

Finish your Google listing

A complete listing tells Google what you are and where you serve. Today it says "print shop" and little else.

One set of hours, everywhereGoogle says you open at 10. Bing says 9.

Google shows weekdays 10 AM to 6 PM. Bing shows a 9 AM opening. The Close at 5 texts assumed pickup ends at 4 PM. Pick the real hours once and put them on Google, Bing, Apple Maps, Facebook and your contact page. Add holiday hours before Thanksgiving.

One thing to know about the name. Google's guidelines say a listing name should match the business's real-world name, and adding words like "DTF Transfers" can get a profile suspended. Lots of DTF shops do it, and it probably helps you rank for "DTF." It's your call. If you change it to "B Family Designs," the description, categories, products and reviews carry the "DTF" signal instead.
