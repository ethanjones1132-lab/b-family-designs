Your real week, in ten seconds.

Drop in your Shopify orders export. You get your Monday numbers, the regulars who went quiet (with messages ready to send), what each product actually earns per inch, and when your orders come in.

Your file stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
Drop your orders export here or tap to pick the .csv file

Getting the file (2 minutes)

  1. In Shopify admin, open Orders and tap Export.
  2. Pick Orders by date. Go back at least 90 days; a year is better.
  3. Choose CSV for Excel (or plain CSV) and tap Export orders.
  4. Shopify emails the file when it's over 50 orders. Download it, then drop it here.

Use Export orders, not "transaction histories."

02

Regulars who went quiet

People who ordered at least twice and haven't been back in a while. They already know you, so one friendly message brings back more of them than any ad. Each draft can be edited. Tap Text or Email to send it from your phone.

Someone counts as quiet once they've been gone longer than the slider, or longer than twice their usual gap between orders, whichever is later. Marketing texts sent by an app need SMS opt-in, so "Not opted in" people get a personal message from you or an email.

03

What you actually earn per inch

Your real sales per inch, product by product, after discounts. Put in your real supply costs and the profit column becomes exact.

Inches: gang sheets count their length; mystery sheets count 72 or 84; transfers by size count their area spread across 22-inch film, plus 15% for spacing. Sales are line prices after order discounts, before tax and shipping. Your time and labor aren't included.

05

Your best accounts

Who spends the most with you. Prepaid fit marks people who ordered four or more times in the last 90 days, the ones a monthly prepaid inch plan would suit.

How the numbers work. Canceled orders are left out. Sales mean order totals minus tax and refunds. Same-day counts shipped orders placed before 2 PM that were fulfilled the same calendar day; pickup orders are left out because Shopify marks them fulfilled when they're picked up. "New" means a customer's first order in this file, so a longer export gives truer numbers.
