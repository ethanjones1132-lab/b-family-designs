Why it's worth building

Team orders are big, repeat every season, and come with a deadline. Today each one means typing names into a layout by hand. Decorators already pay for tools like STAHLS' Team Builder to skip that step. Here, the coach does it for you.

04

How it goes live

No new app and no monthly fee to start. Shopify cart links can carry a roster to checkout, so a hosted page is enough for the first season.

Week 1pilot
  • Host this page and link it from your menu as "Team Orders."
  • Test-print one real roster to check sizes, fonts and colors.
  • Send it to three coaches you already print for.
Week 2–3your look
  • Add your team fonts, outline colors and a logo upload for the front.
  • Set rules for each league, such as football number sizes.
Before springscale
  • Put it inside your Shopify theme as a page.
  • Save each team, so next season the coach just edits the roster.
  • Tell the 71 team and uniform shops on your list it exists.
