Prices checked Oct 2How they're made: your answer

Mystery sheets earn less than half per inch of a custom sheet.

A 72" mystery sheet is $15, which works out to $0.21 an inch. Ready Press DTF in Dalton sells named theme sheets, 22×36 for $18. That's $0.50 an inch, the same as their custom work, with over a hundred themes listed.

Fix · 30 minOne question decides it. If mystery sheets are filled from leftover space or overruns, they're found money; keep them cheap. If they're printed fresh, raise them a few dollars, or show the themes by name the way Ready Press does and price closer to custom.

3
Fact from your storeReorders: your number

56 By Size transfers are still on the old price.

Of the 250 sizes your store lists, 194 cost 7.5¢ a square inch. The other 56 cost 5¢. Almost all of the cheap ones were created between December 22 and February 27, and every size added since March is at 7.5¢. It looks like the price went up and the older sizes kept the old rate. A customer reordering one of those sizes pays a third less than everyone else.

56sizes at 5¢ a square inch
$2.14average shortfall on each of those transfers
$14.72the By Size price on your homepage, which is one of the old sizes

See the largest underpriced sizes

Your homepage card shows "Transfers By Size $14.72" because Shopify shows the first size in the list. That's the 20.72×14.21, which is $22.08 at 7.5¢. Sorting a small size to the top would show "from $0.09" instead.

Fix · 15 minIn Shopify's bulk editor, set the 56 sizes to 7.5¢ a square inch, or change the rate in the gang sheet app if that's where it's set. Then move a small size to the top of the list so the homepage shows the real starting price.

Checked at your checkoutMailer cost: your number

Shipping charges the label, not the mailer.

Your checkout quotes the UPS rate with a $0.00 handling fee: $6.65 to Cartersville or the Birmingham area, $9.25 to Los Angeles. The mailer, backing board and tape come out of your pocket on every shipped order. Your products have no weight set, so one sheet and ten sheets both quote $6.65, even when the bigger order ships in a bigger, heavier box.

$0.00handling fee added to UPS rates
$6.65quoted for a $30 order and for a $300 order alike
0 lbweight on your products
a month back

Fix · 20 minShopify lets you add a flat or percentage handling fee to carrier rates. Set it to what a mailer really costs you. Then give each sheet a weight so big orders quote a fair rate.
