04

How it runs in your Shopify

No new website and no new checkout. Four pieces, set up once: three are already in your Shopify, and the fourth is the Monday report.

Shopify Subscriptions · free appThey join on a Sheet Club page

A "Sheet Club" product with a monthly plan. Shopify bills it on the 1st, and members pause, skip or cancel from their account.

Shopify Flow · built inCredit lands automatically

When the monthly charge goes through, Flow adds the plan's store credit to their account with a 60-day expiry.

Your gang sheet builderThey order like always

Build the sheet, check out, and the credit pays. They just need to be signed in to their account or use Shop Pay.

Your Real WeekYou see it on Monday

Members get a tag, so their orders are flagged for the front of the print queue, and the Monday text shows club sales and credit left.

Store credit can expire on a scheduleShopify lets you set an expiry each time credit is issued, so the 60-day rollover is built in.
Flow can issue the creditFlow's "Send Admin API request" action can add store credit, the same move merchants use for cashback.
Tested before launchWe'd run the whole loop in a free development store first: join, charge, credit, checkout, cancel.
A fallback existsIf the Flow step gives trouble, a membership app can handle the credits for a monthly fee.
05

House rules we'd recommend

Simple enough to fit on the club page. Copy them as a starting point.

  1. Your plan renews on the 1st, and your credit lands the same day.
  2. Credit lasts 60 days, so a slow month rolls into the next one.
  3. Spend it on anything in the store: gang sheets, UV DTF, mystery sheets, pickup or shipped.
  4. Need more mid-month? Grab a top-up at your plan's rate.
  5. Pause, skip or cancel anytime from your account. Credit you've already got stays until it expires.
  6. Founding members keep their price for 12 months.

Why credit that lasts 60 days and not "use it or lose it": regulars are the whole point. The club should feel like a better deal every month, not a trap. Shopify sets the expiry, so nobody tracks it by hand.
